Aleksandra Świerkowska

Technical University of Munich

I am a PhD student at the Systems Research Group at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Prof. Pramod Bhatotia.

My research drives the development of practical quantum systems, with a core focus on fault-tolerant quantum computing. I aim to bridge the gap between theoretical Quantum Error Correction (QEC) and physical quantum hardware through rigorous hardware-software co-design, developing scalable compilation frameworks and robust controller stacks.

Before my PhD, I wrote my master's thesis, "Multi-Objective Circuit Optimizations in the Munich Quantum Software Stack", in cooperation with the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre (LRZ), supervised by Prof. Martin Schulz.

Research Interests

  • Quantum Error Correction
  • Quantum Compilers
  • Quantum Controllers
