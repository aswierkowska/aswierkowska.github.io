## About Me

I’m a PhD student  at the [Systems Research Group](https://dse.in.tum.de/), Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Prof. Pramod Bhatotia. My research drives the development of practical quantum systems, with a core focus on fault-tolerant quantum computing. I aim to bridge the gap between theoretical Quantum Error Correction (QEC) and physical quantum hardware through rigorous hardware-software co-design, developing scalable compilation frameworks and robust controller stacks.


Before that, I did my master's thesis ("Multi-Objective Circuit Optimizations in the Munich Quantum Software Stack") in cooperation with the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre supervised by Prof. Martin Schulz.
